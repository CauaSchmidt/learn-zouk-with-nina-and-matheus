    Partners on stage since 2018, and in the studios of Campinas long before that.

    Their dance spans Zouk, Lambada and Forró, built on years of Classical and Contemporary Ballet, Jazz and Hip-Hop. That mix shapes the way they teach.

    At home they direct Barracão da Dança, a community of more than 320 students in Campinas.

    Nina Darbello

    Teacher, performer and actress

    Born in 1998, Nina started with Classical Ballet at age 5 and now specializes in Zouk, Lambada, High Heels and Lady Style, with a young and creative approach to dancing and teaching.

    Known for her musicality, autonomy and expressiveness, she co-directs Barracão da Dança and holds many national and international titles.

    @ninadarbello on Instagram

    Matheus Franco

    Teacher, performer and co-director of Barracão da Dança

    Born in 1990, Matheus found his passion for dance at 13 and started training at 20, through Classical and Contemporary Ballet, Hip-Hop and Jazz, before finding his calling in Brazilian Zouk.

    Today he teaches alongside Nina at the biggest Zouk and Lambada congresses in the world.

    @thsfranco on Instagram
